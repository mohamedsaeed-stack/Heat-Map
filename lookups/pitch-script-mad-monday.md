# FlapKap Heat · Mad Monday — pitch script

Ten minutes with Sharif, Monday 13 Oct 2026. Deck: https://claude.ai/artifact/2b17urorHq2GQt6zJ8UFgi (16 slides, appendix hidden).
Read it as a reference, not a teleprompter. Times are cumulative.

---

**1 · Cover — 0:00**
"Ten minutes, one idea. It's about how our UAE team sells. It starts with a question, then a story, then a few numbers, then what I'd like us to do next."

**2 · Hook: Pokémon Go — 0:20**
"In 2016, millions of people walked their own streets to catch things that don't exist. 500 million downloads in eight weeks. 8.7 billion kilometres on foot. A hundred million dollars in twenty days."
*(pause)* "People move for a game, for collecting, for rare finds. Hold that thought."

**3 · Hook 2 — 0:55**
"Our clients' neighbours are real. They're one kilometre away. And nobody walks over."
*(let it land)*

**4 · Question — 1:10**
"The last big deal you signed: was it on a call, or across a table?"
*(wait for the answer; almost everyone says across a table)*
"Right. That's the whole pitch."

**5 · Problem / opportunity — 1:40**
"The one thing the UAE team can do that Egypt can't is show up in person. We barely use it.
Cost: the UAE team costs several times the Egypt team, and the only thing unique about them is that they can be in the room.
Trust: a call earns a meeting; a visit earns a relationship and the referrals that come with it.
Speed: one conversation on site clears the questions that stall a sign-up for weeks.
Motivation: visits feel like a chore, and nobody sees the wins."

**6 · Visits — 2:30**
"Here is the honest number. Since March, HubSpot has ten in-person meetings marked as held. Ten, across the whole UAE team, in seven months. Another 38 were booked and never marked as done. So today we can't even tell what a visit is worth, because nobody records it. That's the problem the pilot fixes."

**7 · Social proof is local — 3:10**
"The strongest reference we own is the business next door that already grows with FlapKap. It only works if someone walks in and says so. Every funded client is a reference for its street. A visit turns that into a conversation."

**8 · Meet FlapKap Heat — 3:40**
"So we built Heat. Every client, every deal, every branch, on one map. Green is funded, amber is open, red is lost. Draw an area, it orders the stops by road, one tap opens it in Google Maps. It's live today: 44,914 UAE companies, more than 10,000 placed from their own Google listing, every branch its own pin."
*(open the live map only if he reaches for it)*

**9 · Mad Monday — 4:20**
"And one ritual so it becomes a habit. Mad Monday. Every Monday the UAE team is out: open deals first, funded clients' neighbours for referrals, a coffee with the ones we lost. The map becomes the plan."

**10 · Film — 4:40**
"Let me show you what a Monday looks like." *(click play, say nothing for 40 seconds)*

**11 · Proof — 5:30**
"This is why it works. 80% of our open deals sit within one kilometre of a client we already funded. 93% within two. Even 78% of our lost deals have a happy client around the corner. A visit is a referral walk-in, not a cold call."

**12 · Early signs — 6:10**
"Two things we've already seen. Before any game existed, Yusuf, an SDR, saw where our funded clients are and booked two meetings in two days.
And of the ten companies we did visit, five signed up on the platform afterwards. Of the 1,132 companies we only met by video or phone in the same period, one in five did. Ten is a small sample, and reps may have picked the warmer leads. That's exactly what thirty days of Mondays will tell us."

**13 · Rules — 7:00**
"The game. Five visits on a Monday keeps your streak. Close a deal and the area takes your colour. Most closes in an area this month is Area Dominator. Big open deals sparkle on the map as bonus finds, the rare Pokémon. After 30 days, four numbers: visits per rep per week, meetings booked, pipeline created, deals won, each against the 30 days before."

**14 · Dominator — 7:40**
"Monthly titles: Area Dominator per area, City Dominator for the most areas held. A small fixed prize. Nothing touches commission until we've seen it work."

**15 · Next — 8:00**
"Three things. One: four Mad Mondays with the UAE field team. Two: you launch it with me; it lands differently coming from you. Three: after 30 days the four numbers decide whether we scale it. No budget, no new tools, no process change. Just Mondays."
*(pause, let him react)*

**16 · Close — 8:45**
"More visits. More closes. A team that wants to win. That's it."
*(stop talking)*

## If he asks

- **Does anyone else do this?** "Map-based field sales is standard in FMCG and pharma: Badger Maps, SPOTIO, Map My Customers. Nobody in UAE lending. We built ours on our own CRM for free."
- **Is the data right?** "The map is our CRM plus Google. The visit history is the unreliable part, and that's what Mad Monday fixes. Every number I used today I checked in HubSpot and the admin app."
- **Were those ten visits sales visits or inspections?** "Sales. The admin app records Risk inspections separately; none of the ten is one."
- **What if reps game it?** "Visits are logged with a location. Closes are HubSpot deals. Both are auditable."
- **Why no budget?** "Because I want a yes on the idea, not on a line item. If it works, we'll talk about prizes with numbers in hand."
